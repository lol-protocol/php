Property Division under the Ministry of Economic Affairs is looking at the prospective of
setting up a voluntary registration for copyright works.

6. Copyright registration will be done for the following categories of works:
(a) Original literary, dramatic, musical and artistic works
(b) Cinematographic works; and
(c) Sound recordings
Registration process will be similar for all categories.

perform, broadcast, reproduce etc.

will serve as a prima facie evidence in courts.

B. LEGAL DEPOSIT
27. Yes, Bhutan has a legal deposit system.
28. Legal Deposit Act, 1999.
29. The legal deposit is mandatory. In case of non-compliance, Any publisher who fails to
deposit publications with the National Library and Archives as required by the Legal
Deposit Act shall, upon conviction be liable to a fine of Nu.3,00,000/- which shall be
revised once in every five year. Payment of fine shall not exempt such
person(s)/organization(s).
30. The function of the National Library and Archives are:
(i)
To build up a complete collection of materials, published or produced in any
language, emanating from or relating to Bhutan
(ii)
To analyse, describe, preserve and make available for use its collection of the
materials
(iii) To render bibliographic services and serve as the National Bibliographic Agency
31. No interaction so far.
