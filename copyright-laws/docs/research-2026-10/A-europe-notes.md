General caveats
- No statute text could be opened. Every term figure comes from WIPO Lex catalogue pages (law title, dates, amendment level), national office pages where found, and Wikimedia Commons "Copyright rules by territory" summaries or law-firm guides as the term source. For EU/EEA states life + 70 is also the Directive 2006/116/EC baseline.
- Descriptive fields are "Not specified" wherever results did not confirm them. This is deliberate and covers many moral_rights, orphan_works, digital_protection and fair_use_exceptions cells.
- linked_resources URLs all appeared in search results. Several WIPO Lex IDs are the best match from result titles and snippets, not opened pages. I flag the uncertain ones below.
- `registration_required` is "No" everywhere (Berne no-formalities). The parenthetical "voluntary registration" is added only where results mentioned a voluntary register (AL, AZ, BY, GE, KZ, UA; ME, RS and SI hinted at deposit or recording).

## C1: EU / EEA

### BG Bulgaria - confidence: medium
- Law: Copyright and Related Rights Act (1993, as amended). WIPO lists it as "Law on Copyright and Neighboring Rights".
- Term: Art. 27 life + 70, Art. 28 anonymous from publication (Commons).
- Moral rights: guide sites only. Orphan: Art. 71b, IRIS note (2015).
- Body: Ministry of Culture, Copyright and Related Rights Directorate (IFLA 2012, WIPO reply).
- URL: https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/fr/legislation/details/19460
- Doubt: no statute text opened; WIPO and Commons disagree on the amendment level.

### HR Croatia - confidence: medium
- Law: Copyright and Related Rights Act, NN 111/2021 (WIPO catalogue; Europeana: in force 22 Oct 2021).
- Term: Art. 99 of the 2003 Act (Commons, amended to 127/2014). I did not see the 2021 article, but the 2021 Act is a DSM transposition.
- Orphan: amendments in force Nov 2014 (Directive 2012/28/EU).
- Body: State Intellectual Property Office (DZIV). Only an IFLA 2013 report calling it the "State Institute for IP" supports this; the Ministry of Culture and Media may also hold copyright duties.
- URL: https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/details.jsp?id=15476 (WIPO Lex entry for the Croatian copyright act; marked superseded, it points to the newer entry).
- Doubt: the enforcement body, and that the link is the earlier-act record.

### CY Cyprus - confidence: medium-low
- Law: Copyright Law 1976 (Law 59/1976, as amended). The exact current English title is uncertain: Wikisource says "Copyright Law, 1976", while older WIPO texts say "Copyright and Related Rights".
- Term: life + 70 (University of Cyprus Library page, Commons). Related rights 50 years.
- Body: Minister of Commerce and Industry is the competent authority (UCY page). The ministry has been renamed since, and no dedicated "Copyright Office" was found.
- WIPO consolidation covers amendments to 77(I)/2019 (a different listing says 2022).
- URL: https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/fr/legislation/details/19025
- Doubt: title wording, ministry name, moral/orphan unconfirmed.

### EE Estonia - confidence: high

- Body: Justiits- ja Digiministeerium (Ministry of Justice and Digital Affairs), from the Riigi Teataja amendment history (from 1 Jan 2025).
- URL: https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/22138
- Doubt: none significant.

### LT Lithuania - confidence: medium-high

### MT Malta - confidence: medium

## C2: Europe / Central Asia

### UA Ukraine - confidence: medium-high
- Law: Law No. 2811-IX of 1 Dec 2022 (in force 1 Jan 2023); it replaced the 1993 Law. WIPO has a consolidated text dated 12 Dec 2025 with no English version.
- Term: life + 70 (Chambers 2025 guide, Commons, Wikipedia). Moral rights indefinite (Chambers, EIFL).
- Orphan, TPM Art. 53 and exceptions: EIFL comments.
- Body: Ministry of Economy (Order No. 11319 of 16 Aug 2023 on state registration); the ministry was reorganised in 2025, so the name may differ. The National Office for IP and Innovations is also named.
- URL: https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/22385 (the ID could be the 2023 or the newest record).
- Doubt: voluntary state registration is inferred from the Order and Art. 9(5), not stated outright.

### RS Serbia - confidence: medium-high
- Law: Official Gazette 104/2009, amended 99/2011, 119/2012, 29/2016, 66/2019.
- Term: Art. 102 life + 70; anonymous from lawful publication (Commons, Chambers 2026).
- Orphan: current law has an "Anonymous and Orphan Works" section; a 2026 draft law would align it with Directive 2012/28/EU.
- Body: Intellectual Property Office (zis.gov.rs). Recording and deposit of works comes from a WIPO survey reply, which is old.
- URL: https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/es/legislation/details/19376 (presumed 2019 consolidation).
- Doubt: whether the 2026 draft was enacted.

### BY Belarus - confidence: medium-low

- Body: NCIP, a collective management organisation that also offers voluntary registration and deposit.
- Digital: only old IIPA material (1998 law) was seen, so "Not specified".
