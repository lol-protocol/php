The CPA will become effective on July 1, 2023. Colorado becomes the latest state to join the US Privacy Landscape with its own comprehensive privacy law following the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (CDPA), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).
