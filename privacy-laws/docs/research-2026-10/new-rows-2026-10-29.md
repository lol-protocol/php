# Privacy laws: follow-up on unresolved places (2026-10-29)

Web search only; nothing was read in the official text.

## Added (1)

- BS · Data Protection (Privacy of Personal Information) Act 2003 — assented 2003-04-11, in force 2007-04-02 (S.I. 25/2007). A Data Protection Bill 2025 to replace it is still prospective.

## Still not added

- WS Samoa and TV Tuvalu: no general law found (low confidence; based on absence of evidence in PacLII and aggregators, not proof that none exists).
- DJ Djibouti: Digital Code, Book I, adopted by the National Assembly on 2025-06-30; no promulgation date, law number or entry into force found.
- TO Tonga (Privacy Act 2025, Act 34 of 2025), KI Kiribati (Data Protection Act 2025), MH Marshall Islands (Personal Data Protection Act 2025, P.L. 2025-43): enacted or reported as enacted, but no assent or commencement date found.

## Official texts read on 31 October 2026

The session's own network does not reach the official sites, so a throwaway workflow on a scratch branch downloaded the texts on a CI runner and printed the passages that mention the deceased, research and genetic data. Read this way: El Salvador (Decree 144 of 2024), Chile (Law 21.719, from the Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional), Somalia (Data Protection Act 2023), Gabon (Law 025/2023), Mali (Law 2013-015, machine translation) and the WIPO Lex profiles of Equatorial Guinea and the Marshall Islands.

- `deceased_data`, `research_exemption` and `genetic_data` of those five laws now cite articles from the text itself (El Salvador, Chile and Somalia with high confidence; Gabon and Mali medium, because the Gabon PDF has two columns and Mali is a machine translation).
- Somalia: the earlier value said the Act has no research exemption; the text has one as a lawful basis (art. 14(2)(k)). Genetic data are not in its sensitive-data list (art. 2(15)).
- Not reached: Niger (scanned PDF without text), Guinea (scanned PDF), Burundi and Mauritania (no copy found), PacLII (Samoa, Tuvalu, Tonga, Kiribati, Marshall Islands: blocked with 403 for the runner too) and the Djibouti sites (no hit for the Digital Code).
- Copyright: WIPO Lex lists for Equatorial Guinea only the 1889 Civil Code under "Copyright and Related Rights", and for the Marshall Islands only the 1991 Unauthorized Copies of Recorded Materials Act; both stay out of the dataset.
