## Notable features

The Act is identified as the Data Protection Act, 2011 and numbered Act No. 5 of 2012; the accessible text states commencement on publication. It creates a Data Protection Commission, but current government-linked reporting says the Commission has not yet been established.

## FAQ

What rights were located in Lesotho's Data Protection Act?+

The supplied research supports access, qualified correction or deletion, objection, and direct-marketing opt-out rights, including a fourteen-day correction-outcome timeline.

Is Lesotho's Data Protection Commission operational?+

The Act creates a Data Protection Commission, but current government-linked reporting says it has not yet been established; an operational general complaint endpoint was not located.

Is the credit-reporting route a general data-broker opt-out?+

No. The separate credit-reporting route should be kept distinct from a general privacy or data-broker removal route.
