# Copyright

The Agency for Cultural Affairs establishes the Copyright Subdivision of the Culture Council to discuss how to improve the copyright system, in response to social change and the development and popularization of information technologies. We also carry out various measures to improve the accessibility and availability of copyrighted works, and engages in education programs on copyright issues. We also address international challenges in copyright protection by setting ground rules for piracy and copyright issues overseas.
