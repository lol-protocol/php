What is the BDPA About?
1) Introduces right to informational privacy
2) Compliance obligations for controllers and processors

3) Passage in 3rd Q 2019 | Proclaimed in 1st Q 2021

4) Extraterritorial in scope (affects processing outside 🇧🇧)

5) Informed consent required to process sensitive data
6) Breach notification within 72 hours

7) Fines up to BDS $500,000.00
