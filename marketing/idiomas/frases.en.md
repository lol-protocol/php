## Long text (manifesto)
Every human being who walked this world before us had dreams, loves, fears, plans, and people they didn't want to lose. They were once children imagining their future; then they grew up, fought for something, loved someone… but one day they became a memory.

Thousands of years have passed, millions of stories, and time has never stopped for anyone.

It is our turn now, this small moment in eternity.

Perhaps that is why life is not about how much time we have, but about what we do with the brief instant we were given to exist.
