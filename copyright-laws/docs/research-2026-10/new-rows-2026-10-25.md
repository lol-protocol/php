## Notes on this batch

- Terms come mostly from Wikimedia Commons summaries, not from the statutes. Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (life + 75) and Timor-Leste (life + 50, Code of 2022) are the weakest.
- Dominica (Act 5 of 2003, s. 11) and Saint Kitts and Nevis (Act 14 of 2024, extended from 50 years, not retroactive; commencement not seen) have life + 70; Maldives raised its term to 70 in 2024 and joined Berne in 2025 (in force 22 November 2025).
- Congo and Equatorial Guinea are OAPI members (Bangui Agreement, Annex VII also applies); Djibouti is not and uses its own Law 154/AN/06.
- Turkmenistan: the 2012 law was superseded by a version amended in 2017 (Law No. 668-V); the term comes from the 2012 text.
- Not added: Eswatini (Act 4 of 2018) and Equatorial Guinea, because no general term of protection was found.
- WCT and WPPT memberships could not be confirmed for most of these places, so they are not listed.
- Brunei already had a row and was not touched.
